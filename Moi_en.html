About me

I’m studying innovation project management at IRIIG in Lyon and looking for an 18–24 month apprenticeship. I interview users and potential partners to understand how they work, compare options and estimate what a first test would cost.

At Dive Lyon, I built tools for the people receiving orders and checking operational data. I started as an intern in 2024 and returned as a subcontractor in 2025. See my experience at Dive.

Running GPU machines led me to explore a question of my own: could a producer use locally undervalued electricity to sell computing capacity? I’m investigating it through DCR Solutions.
